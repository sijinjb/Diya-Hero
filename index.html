Welcome to Diya Hero

Your Trusted Hero MotoCorp Dealer in Kerala

At Diya Hero, we take immense pride in being the Authorized Dealer of Hero MotoCorp in Kollam and Thiruvananthapuram Districts. Since our journey began in March 2007 at Kollam and expanded to Thiruvananthapuram in March 2016, we have been committed to providing our customers with an exceptional vehicle purchase experience.

Our dedication to customer satisfaction, coupled with top-notch sales and service facilities, has made us one of the leading Hero dealerships in Kerala. We strive to bring the best of Hero MotoCorp to our customers, ensuring reliability, quality, and trust at every step.

Why Choose Diya Hero?

  • Unmatched Sales & Service:
    • A large service center capable of handling 100+ vehicles daily.
    • Skilled and experienced staff dedicated to customer care and vehicle maintenance.
  • Comprehensive Network:
    • Authorized outlets strategically located across key locations in Kollam and Thiruvananthapuram districts.
  • Outstanding Customer Satisfaction:
    • Expanded to neighboring districts due to the loyalty and support of our satisfied customers.
    • Regular free safety classes for customers, ensuring safer and trouble-free driving experiences.
