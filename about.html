Building Trust Since 2007

Diya Hero is the Authorized Hero Motocorp dealer in Kollam and Thiruvananthapuram Districts. We started our business with Hero Motocorp in March 2007 at Kollam and in March 2016 at Thiruvananthapuram.

About Diya Hero

We are committed to providing an excellent purchase experience to our customers. With a huge sales and service section, experienced and dedicated staff, and a service area capable of handling more than 100 vehicles daily, we have become one of the top automobile dealerships in Kerala.

Diya Hero has authorized networks in important centres in both districts and has extended its business to neighboring districts due to outstanding performance and customer satisfaction.

Award-Winning Performance

Hero MotoCorp has awarded Diya Hero for its excellent performance in sales, service, and spares. We maintain a stock of more than 1000 vehicles, offering a wide range of selections.

Our professional staff ensures a smooth buying process and offers test rides to give customers a feel of the product. We strictly follow the first-come, first-serve policy to ensure timely delivery of vehicles.
